# Ward corpus

The estate's identity service. The repo directory is still `wzd_auth`; the
service is **Ward**.

The project's knowledge and work, as an LLM-maintained wiki. Start here.

- [routing.md](routing.md) — which layer answers which kind of question.
- [log.md](log.md) — append-only record of every meaningful change.
- [briefs/](briefs/) — work specs: [todo](briefs/todo/) · [done](briefs/done/) · [superseded](briefs/superseded/)
- [todos/](todos/) — captured ideas, pre-spec.

**Retrieval budget:** read this page, then **at most 2–3 wiki pages**. Triage on
the summaries below — that is what they are for. Needing a fourth page is a
signal a page must split, not a licence to keep reading.

> **Ward is deployed, and so is the redesign.** On 2026-10-13 the owner moved
> the estate to local accounts in every app plus "Sign in with Ward" over
> OpenID Connect ([wiki/decisions-oidc.md](wiki/decisions-oidc.md)), built in
> briefs 18–29 and cut over the same day. Brief 30 then retired the old
> shared-cookie contract ([wiki/status.md](wiki/status.md)). Read the
> decisions before proposing anything: most obvious alternatives were
> considered and rejected there, with reasons.

New here? [wiki/overview.md](wiki/overview.md) for the settled shape in a
paragraph, then [wiki/estate.md](wiki/estate.md) for what it has to absorb.
