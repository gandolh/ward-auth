Ward is the estate's identity service. It replaces six separately-designed auth
stacks with **one identity, one credential store, and one set of access
grants** — a Fastify + SQLite service at `/ward-api`, and the one login page in
the estate at `/ward`.

<dl className="ward-facts">
  <dt>Runs at</dt>
  <dd><code>/ward</code> (UI) and <code>/ward-api</code> (API) on the production site, one origin, no subdomains</dd>
  <dt>Owns</dt>
  <dd>Credentials, account identity, and <code>(subject, app, roles)</code> grants</dd>
  <dt>Does not own</dt>
  <dd>Anything an app stores about a person — apps keep their own rows, keyed on an opaque <strong>subject</strong></dd>
  <dt>Consumed by</dt>
  <dd>atrium · public-resource-map · newspapper · sports-app</dd>
  <dt>Signs with</dt>
  <dd>One Ed25519 key, held by Ward alone; apps get the public half over JWKS to verify ID tokens</dd>
</dl>

## The shape in a paragraph

Every app keeps its own accounts and its own login page. "Sign in with Ward" is
a second button on that page: an OpenID Connect code flow with PKCE, against
Ward as the provider. Ward checks the password once, keeps the session as a
**30-day rotating refresh family**, and hands the app an ID token naming the
person's opaque subject, their role in that app, and the Ward session
(`ward_sid`). The app makes its own session from that. While it lasts, the app
asks Ward at most every 30 seconds whether the Ward session is still live
(`POST /session-status`), so a Ward sign-out or a console revoke ends it
everywhere within 30 seconds. Ward's own cookies stay under `/ward-api` and are
never sent to an app.

## What this site is made of

Two sources, kept apart on purpose, because they go stale in different ways.

- **Authored** — the pages above. Written by hand against the code, and the
  place where architecture, the HTTP surface and the schema are described. The
  corpus has long recorded that it had no `architecture.md`, `api.md` or
  `data.md`; these are those pages.
- **Rendered from the corpus** — everything under *Deep dive* and *Status*. Those
  are generated on every build from `corpus/`, which is the project's own
  decision record. They carry a banner saying so. Edit the corpus, not the copy.
The diagrams are compiled by [archify](https://github.com/tt-a1i/archify) from
typed JSON in `docs/diagrams/` and validated before they ship.

:::note[In production]
Ward has been deployed at `/ward` on the production site since 2026-10-04, and
atrium, newspapper, public-resource-map and sports-app sign in through it.
They moved to "Sign in with Ward" on 2026-10-13, and the old shared-cookie
contract (`/introspect`, app keys, the access cookie at `Path=/`) was retired
the same day (brief 30). imbatranimOS was a fifth consumer until 2026-10-07,
when it began signing people in itself. Local tests still cannot show the
cookie paths, because `handle_path` strips a prefix that nothing local strips;
the deployed Ward is where they run for real. See [Status](/wiki/status/).
:::
